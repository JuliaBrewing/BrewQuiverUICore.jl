BrewQuiverUICore.jl

The frontend-neutral core of the user interfaces of BrewQuiver.jl, the engine that models brewing as a quiver: a directed acyclic multigraph whose nodes are the contents of vessels at the stages of the brewing, and whose arrows are the processes between them.

BrewQuiverUICore holds what every interface needs and nothing that draws: plain Julia data and functions, with no terminal or graphics dependencies, shared by the terminal interface BrewQuiverTerm.jl and the graphical one, BrewQuiverApp.jl.

  • an editing session: a quiver, its file, its latest brew, the selection, and the nodes that changed in the last step;
  • edit commands that validate themselves, refuse what would break the quiver or its brew, and record what reverts them exactly;
  • an undo and redo history, with transactions, merging of consecutive small edits, a bounded length and a saved marker;
  • view data: summaries of nodes and arrows with their results in units, formatting and parsing of quantities, display colors, tables, the timeline;
  • a layered layout of the quiver, and the convention of the data that interfaces keep in the files of quivers;
  • an ingredient catalog, for adding lots.

Installation

The package is under development and not registered. It takes BrewQuiver and its siblings from GitHub through the [sources] of its Project.toml (Julia 1.11 or later):

using Pkg
Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/JuliaBrewing/BrewQuiverUICore.jl")

A first look

using BrewQuiverUICore, BrewQuiver, Unitful, UnitfulBrewing
include(joinpath(pkgdir(BrewQuiverUICore), "docs", "quivers.jl"))   # single_batch()

s = Session(single_batch())
node_summary(s, :wort).gravity
1.053188386534343 sg

An edit is a command applied to the session; the brew follows, from the nodes it affects, and the nodes whose results changed are known (those downstream of the malt, not the hops):

apply!(s, SetAmount(:pale, 4.8u"kg"))
format(node_summary(s, :wort).gravity), length(changed(s)), :cascade in changed(s)
("1.056 sg", 11, false)

and it can be undone:

undo_description(s)
"Set :pale to 4.8 kg"
undo!(s)
format(node_summary(s, :wort).gravity)
"1.053 sg"

An edit after which the quiver would no longer brew is refused, and changes nothing:

try
    apply!(s, SetAmount(:strike_water, 1u"L"))
catch e
    sprint(showerror, e)
end
"Set :strike_water to 1 L: the quiver would no longer brew: not enough liquid to drain: 3.23 L available, 4.8 L retained by the grain and the vessel"
